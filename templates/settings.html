ntfy.sh Push Notifications Setup Guide

Follow these 4 simple steps to receive real-time pond alerts on your phone or PC

WHAT IS NTFY.SH?

ntfy.sh (pronounced notify) is a 100% free, private push notification service. It connects to ShrimpGuard to deliver instant push notifications, water parameter readings, and AI actionable solutions directly to your phone whenever water conditions turn Warning or Critical. Zero account sign-up is required!

STEP 1

Create or Generate Your Private Alert Topic

A topic name acts like a secret private channel for your pond. Click "Generate Secure Topic" in Settings to create an unguessable private topic name (e.g. sg-pond-8a3f9c), then click "Save Profile".

Your Current Alert Topic:
sg-pond-...
STEP 2

Where to Find ntfy.sh (Get the App or Open Web)

Install the official free app on your phone, or open the web version on your computer:

Android Devices Google Play Store →
iOS / iPhone / iPad Apple App Store →
Desktop / Laptop Open ntfy.sh Website →
STEP 3

Subscribe to Your Exact Topic in ntfy

  1. Open the ntfy app on your mobile phone (or visit https://ntfy.sh in your browser).
  2. Tap or click the "+" (Subscribe to topic) button.
  3. Paste or type your exact Alert Topic from Step 1.
  4. Leave the default server as https://ntfy.sh.
  5. Tap "Subscribe". Ensure system notification permission is set to "Allow" so alerts ring with sound!
STEP 4

Verify with a Test Push Notification

Back in ShrimpGuard Settings, click the blue "Send Test Push Notification" button. You will instantly receive a chime and notification banner on your phone and browser:
