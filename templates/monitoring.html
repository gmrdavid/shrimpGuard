This level (0.0224 ppm) is lower than the acceptable level (<0.03 ppm), hence no corrective action would need to be taken.

Sensitivity Insight: At 28°C, an increase in pH of only +0.2 (to 7.8) yields 0.0344 ppm, which exceeds the acceptable limit (<0.03 ppm) and requires immediate aeration & mitigation.
